Lucinda Glasc is the last surviving [[Cremators|Cremator]], one of the woodsworn created under [[Ossua]] within the faction [[Sub Rosa]]. She is the author of [[Among the Woodsworn]], a firsthand account of the Cremators' history and downfall[^woodsworn].

## Among the Cremators
Like her sisters, Glasc was built tall, thin, and elegant, with a mouthless porcelain mask and a body of marble rather than wood, her name ending in -a in keeping with Cremator convention[^woodsworn]. She served under [[Ossua]] performing what she describes as "funerals for the living" — elaborate ceremonies in which condemned targets were bound and their loved ones made to grieve before the end[^woodsworn].

## Witness to the Hunt
Following [[Ossua]]'s rebellion and death at Hiebrief's hand, the [[Rose Troupe]] was ordered to hunt down the remaining Cremators. Glasc records watching her sisters killed one by one, unable to stop it, and survived as the sole exception[^woodsworn].

## Saoglas
After [[Hiebrief]]'s disappearance and the [[Wake of Steel]], Glasc followed [[Fatuo]] and the remaining woodsworn out of Edelith's walls to [[Saoglas]]. She was present when the survivors destroyed Hiebrief's avatar together, tearing it apart by hand — an act she describes as "necessary" and "right," and the most unified she ever saw the group[^woodsworn]. She later spent a full day and night speaking with [[Rotcio]] and [[Avao Woodsworn|Avao]] before Rotcio's departure, recalling the three of them as "always much happier together"[^woodsworn].

## Departure
Glasc left Saoglas before its end, as corrosion — attributed by the remaining woodsworn to an outside woman who had been taken in — spread through the settlement. She states there was "nothing left that I could save," and describes herself as "too old and too tired to pretend otherwise"[^woodsworn].

## Among the Woodsworn
Glasc wrote her account because, in her words, "there are very few of us left who were there," and "because Ossua deserved someone to remember her"[^woodsworn].
