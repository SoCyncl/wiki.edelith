Ixnael is a temperate world orbited by a single station, [Coatl](coatl.html "A defense and trade station in low orbit"), which handles most off-world trade for the region.

The planet's climate bands were first surveyed by early colonial expeditions[^1], and later re-confirmed by an automated satellite pass[^2].

[apocrypha]
Some elders claim Ixnael was originally home to a second, smaller moon that was lost during an early orbital mishap. No physical evidence supports this, but the story persists in local oral history.
[/apocrypha]

## History

The first settlement was founded along the equatorial coast, chosen for its mild year-round temperatures and easy access to the surrounding waters.
