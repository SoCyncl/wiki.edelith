The Lustrous Expanse is the campaign setting for this wiki's ongoing Dungeons & Dragons campaign. This wiki documents the world, its inhabitants, history, and the events of each session.

The campaign is primarily set in Edelith, a city within the nation of Solstitheo. The Expanse is a vast continent of at least 14 nations, defined by rivers, archipelagos, and fragmented mountain ranges.

About This Wiki [edit]

This wiki is a living document maintained by the players. It records session summaries, tracks characters (player and non-player alike), documents factions and locations, and preserves lore. All template pages in this wiki can be duplicated and filled in for new entries.

Geography [edit]

The Expanse contains at least 14 nations, including the Kingdom of Belkinus, the Kingdom of Elynthi, the Yuan-ti World Dominion, Old Diosia, and Solstitheo — home to the city of Edelith, where the campaign takes place.

The Lustrous Expanse appears largely flat, its geography defined by rivers, great lakes, and scattered archipelagos. A broken mountain range runs from the southeastern coast to Belkinus. Forests concentrate in Elynthi and the northern reaches.

Peoples and Cultures [edit]

The Expanse is home to the full range of races found in D&D 5th Edition, as well as plantkin — a race unique to this setting. Nations vary widely in their openness: Belkinus is cosmopolitan and inclusive, while the Yuan-ti World Dominion is deeply xenophobic.

Magic [edit]

Magic follows D&D 5th Edition rules with one critical exception: resurrection is near-impossible. It requires extraordinary cost or circumstance, and the 9th-level spell true resurrection has not been discovered in the Expanse. Divine magic — as wielded by clerics and paladins — manifests from the caster's personal conviction rather than pure faith in a deity.
