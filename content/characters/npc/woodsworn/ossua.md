Ossua was one of two Mask Makers created by the hand of the Goddess [[Hiebrief]], alongside [[Fatuo]]. She was made deliberately as an extreme — built for Sorrow, an emotion so total it crowded out thought, so that she could never reach full awareness of what she was[^woodsworn]. Like Fatuo, she was not a true angel, but a construction of Hiebrief's own making, connected by strings only the Goddess held, and subservient by design[^woodsworn].

## The Cremators
Ossua led the Cremators, one of two orders housed within the faction [[Sub Rosa]], the other being the [[Rose Troupe]] under [[Fatuo]]. Her people were built as extremes of elegance and fragility: tall, thin, mouthless, with porcelain masks and bodies of marble rather than wood. All were female-presenting, their names ending in -a[^woodsworn].

Unlike the Rose Troupe's quick kills, the Cremators performed what [[Lucinda Glasc]], the last Cremator, called "funerals for the living": elaborate ceremonies in which a bound target's loved ones were made to witness and grieve before the target's death, the coffin buried with the victim left alive inside long enough to know they were missed[^woodsworn]. Only the highest-class targets, those Hiebrief deemed worth the ceremony, were given to the Cremators; everyone else went to the Rose Troupe[^woodsworn].

## Bond with Fatuo
:::source[Lucinda Glasc, Among the Woodsworn]
Hiebrief grew comfortable with them, with Ossua and Fatuo, the way one grows comfortable with a thing they believe cannot surprise them... The two grew close in the way that opposites sometimes do, drawn to each other precisely because neither could fully understand the other's half.
:::
Despite being built as opposing extremes — Sorrow and Joy — Ossua and Fatuo grew close, and for a time spoke of better lives, though they could never fully bridge the distance between their two halves[^woodsworn].

## Rebellion and death
Ossua chose to rebel against Hiebrief, attempting to untangle herself from the strings that controlled her and to find something better for the Cremators in her keeping[^woodsworn]. Hiebrief answered by sending her own avatar to perform on Ossua the same ceremony Ossua had performed on so many others: her porcelain mask was displayed, mourned, and cremated[^woodsworn]. In the aftermath, the Rose Troupe was ordered to hunt down the remaining Cremators one by one, until [[Lucinda Glasc]] alone remained[^woodsworn].

## Legacy
[[Fatuo]] is said to have entered the [[Umbra]] in part searching for a way to bring Ossua back, an act that led to the emergence of the entity now known as [[Miswak]][^woodsworn]. Glasc records writing her account, [[Among the Woodsworn]], in part "because Ossua deserved someone to remember her"[^woodsworn].
